Cheddar Vox Terms of Use

Updated 6 October 2026

These terms cover the Cheddar Vox annotation tool and dataset-services enquiries.

Your account

Provide accurate account information and keep your sign-in details secure. Use an account you are authorised to access.

Your footage and annotations

You retain ownership of your uploaded footage and annotations.

Only upload material you have permission to use. Obtain any necessary permissions from people whose personal information appears in your footage.

You give Cheddar Vox permission to store and process submitted material as needed to provide the service.

Using the tool

Use Cheddar Vox lawfully. Do not upload unlawful material, attempt to access another person’s workspace, interfere with the service or bypass access controls.

Review your results

Voice recognition and annotation suggestions can make mistakes. Check saved labels, timestamps, object outlines and exported data before relying on them.

Features marked “Pending” are not available. Trial examples do not establish general model accuracy.

Disqualified annotations remain recoverable and are excluded from the relevant exports.

Availability and saved work

Cheddar Vox is under active development. Features may change, and interruptions can occur.

Export important work regularly. Saved work is not a substitute for keeping your original footage and your own backups.

Dataset services

Submitting an enquiry does not place an order or authorise a charge. Scope, price, timing, permissions and delivery requirements must be agreed separately before work begins.

Contact

For support or questions about these terms, contact us through the dataset enquiry form.

We will show the updated date when these terms change.
