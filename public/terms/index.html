Cheddar Vox Terms of Use

Updated 24 October 2026

These terms cover Cheddar Vox voice-led video annotation, Video Feedback and dataset-services enquiries.

Your account

Provide accurate account information and keep your sign-in details secure. Use an account you are authorised to access.

Your footage, annotations and feedback

You retain ownership of your uploaded footage, annotations and feedback.

Only upload material you have permission to use. Obtain any necessary permissions from people whose personal information appears in your footage or recordings.

You give Cheddar Vox permission to store and process submitted material as needed to provide the service.

Using the tool

Use Cheddar Vox lawfully. Do not upload unlawful material, attempt to access another person’s workspace, interfere with the service or bypass access controls.

Review your results

Voice recognition can make mistakes. Check saved labels, feedback text, timestamps and exported data before relying on them.

Disqualified video annotations remain recoverable and are excluded from the relevant exports.

Retired tools and existing work

The drawing and image-annotation screens are retired. Removing these screens does not delete existing records or drafts stored in your browser.

Used the old drawing tools? Email us and we'll export your work.

We will need to establish which work belongs to you before providing an export. If work was never saved online, keep the original browser and device available and do not clear its storage; we cannot retrieve browser-only drafts from our database.

Availability and saved work

Cheddar Vox is under active development. Features may change, and interruptions can occur.

Export important work regularly. Saved work is not a substitute for keeping your original footage and your own backups.

Dataset services

Submitting an enquiry does not place an order or authorise a charge. Scope, price, timing, permissions and delivery requirements must be agreed separately before work begins.
