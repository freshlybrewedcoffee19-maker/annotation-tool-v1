Cheddar Vox Privacy Policy

Updated 24 October 2026

Cheddar Vox provides voice-led video annotation and a separate Video Feedback workflow.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we save your name, email address and optional phone number. Google sign-in provides basic account information, such as your name, email address and profile picture. It does not give Cheddar Vox access to your Gmail messages or Google Drive files.

For video annotation, we save uploaded videos, video details, labels, marked time intervals and review decisions. Video Feedback also stores feedback text, marked clips, video revisions, invitations, replies and project activity. Where supported, saved feedback can include the original voice recording, as described below.

We record product-use events such as recording attempts, successful saves and clip plays, with browser visitor and session identifiers and relevant account or workspace references. These event records do not contain the spoken feedback text or audio.

When you request dataset services, we save the contact details and project requirements you submit.

How we use information

We use this information to sign you in, restore your saved work, remember preferences, provide annotation, feedback and export tools, measure how the product is used, and respond to support and dataset enquiries.

Where information is processed

Convex provides our account system and database. Uploaded files may be stored in Convex file storage or private Cloudflare R2 storage. Video Feedback videos and saved voice recordings use private R2 storage. Cloudflare also serves the website and public demonstration files. Google handles Google sign-in.

These providers may process connection and operational information under their own privacy policies.

Microphone use

Microphone access starts when you enable voice controls and grant browser permission.

Cheddar Vox uses your browser’s speech recognition service to interpret commands and speech. Depending on the browser, audio may be sent to its speech recognition provider. Voice recognition is not guaranteed to run entirely on your device.

The video-annotation workflow saves recognised labels and timestamps without recording the original microphone audio. Video Feedback can save the original recording alongside the text on supported desktop browsers. Android and iPhone feedback uses text-only capture. The Video Feedback sample keeps practice feedback in the current tab.

You can turn the microphone off and use manual controls.

Browser storage

We use browser storage to remember your workspace, sign-in state, selected video, interface preferences, product-use identifiers and unfinished work. Queued feedback may include text and recordings awaiting a successful save. Clearing browser storage can remove unsaved drafts and access to an unsigned guest workspace. Work successfully saved to a signed-in account remains associated with that account.

Access and retention

Account-owned workspaces require sign-in. Active Video Feedback review links allow anyone with the link to view the shared video and feedback until the owner revokes access. Invited editors must sign in to upload revisions. Authorised service operators and infrastructure providers may access information when needed to operate, support or secure the service.

Disqualifying a video annotation keeps it available for correction; it does not delete the record. In the annotation workspace, projects and videos moved to Trash can be restored for seven days, after which their files and annotations are permanently removed. Finalising a Video Feedback project can delete older video files after the owner's confirmation; the latest video, feedback and saved voice recordings are retained. Expired temporary uploads are also cleaned up.

Retired tools and retained work

The drawing and image-annotation screens are retired. Existing image labels, drawings, object-related records and browser drafts are retained; retiring the screens does not delete them. The existing deletion controls described above still apply.

Used the old drawing tools? Email us and we'll export your work.

We will need to establish which work belongs to you before providing an export. Browser-only drafts remain on the original device and are not available from our database. Keep that browser's storage intact if you need help recovering them.

Your choices

You can edit annotations or feedback, export saved work, use the available deletion controls and disconnect Google access through your Google account settings.

For account deletion, other uploaded-data deletion requests or help recovering old work, email riya.shah@cheddarvox.com.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, email riya.shah@cheddarvox.com.

We will update this policy when our data practices change and show the updated date here.
