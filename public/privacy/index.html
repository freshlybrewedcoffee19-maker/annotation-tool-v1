Cheddar Vox Privacy Policy

Updated 6 October 2026

Cheddar Vox helps you label, review and export video annotations.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we save your name, email address and optional phone number. Google sign-in provides basic account information, such as your name, email address and profile picture. It does not give Cheddar Vox access to your Gmail messages or Google Drive files.

When you use the annotation tool, we save uploaded videos, video details, labels, metadata, object outlines, visibility decisions, action intervals and review decisions.

When you request dataset services, we save the contact details and project requirements you submit.

How we use information

We use this information to sign you in, restore your saved work, remember preferences, provide annotation and export tools, and respond to dataset enquiries.

Where information is processed

Convex provides our account system, database and storage for uploaded videos. Cloudflare serves the website and public demonstration files. Google handles Google sign-in.

These providers may process connection and operational information under their own privacy policies.

Microphone use

Microphone access starts when you enable voice controls and grant browser permission.

Cheddar Vox uses your browser’s speech recognition service to interpret commands. Depending on the browser, audio may be sent to its speech recognition provider. Voice recognition is not guaranteed to run entirely on your laptop.

Cheddar Vox does not currently save microphone audio recordings in its database. Annotation commands can create saved labels, timestamps and other annotation records.

You can turn the microphone off and use manual controls.

Browser storage

We use browser storage to remember your workspace, sign-in state, selected video and interface preferences. Clearing browser storage can remove access to an unsigned guest workspace. Signed-in work remains associated with your account.

Access and retention

Account-owned workspaces require sign-in. Authorised service operators and infrastructure providers may access information when needed to operate, support or secure the service.

Saved work does not currently have an automatic deletion schedule. Disqualifying an annotation keeps it available for correction; it does not delete the record.

Your choices

You can edit annotations, delete individual action segments, export your work and disconnect Google access through your Google account settings.

To request account or uploaded-data deletion, contact riyashah815@gmail.com. Full account and video deletion are not currently available as self-service buttons.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, contact riyashah815@gmail.com.

We will update this policy when our data practices change and show the updated date here.
