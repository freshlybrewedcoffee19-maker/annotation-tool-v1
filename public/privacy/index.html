Cheddar Vox Privacy Policy

Updated 3 November 2026

Cheddar Vox provides voice-led video annotation. The separate Video Feedback workflow is paused; this policy continues to cover its retained data.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we save your name, email address and optional phone number. Google sign-in provides basic account information, such as your name, email address and profile picture. It does not give Cheddar Vox access to your Gmail messages or Google Drive files.

For video annotation, we save uploaded videos, video details, labels, marked time intervals and review decisions. Previously saved Video Feedback work includes feedback text, marked clips, video revisions, invitations, replies and project activity. Where supported during capture, saved feedback can include the original voice recording, as described below.

We record product-use events such as recording attempts, successful saves and clip plays, with browser visitor and session identifiers and relevant account or workspace references. These event records do not contain the spoken feedback text or audio.

When you request dataset services, we save the contact details and project requirements you submit.

How we use information

We use this information to sign you in, restore your saved work, remember preferences, provide annotation and export tools, retain saved feedback, measure how the product is used, and respond to support and dataset enquiries.

Where information is processed

Convex provides our account system and database. Uploaded files may be stored in Convex file storage or private Cloudflare R2 storage. Video Feedback videos and saved voice recordings use private R2 storage. Cloudflare also serves the website and public demonstration files. Google handles Google sign-in.

These providers may process connection and operational information under their own privacy policies.

Microphone use

Microphone access starts when you enable voice controls and grant browser permission.

Cheddar Vox uses your browser’s speech recognition service to interpret commands and speech. Depending on the browser, audio may be sent to its speech recognition provider. Voice recognition is not guaranteed to run entirely on your device.

The video-annotation workflow saves recognised labels and timestamps without recording the original microphone audio. Before it was paused, Video Feedback could save the original recording alongside the text on supported desktop browsers. Android and iPhone feedback used text-only capture. The Video Feedback sample kept practice feedback in the current tab, not in the account database.

You can turn the microphone off and use manual controls.

Browser storage

We use browser storage to remember your workspace, sign-in state, selected video, interface preferences, product-use identifiers and unfinished work. Previously queued feedback may include unsaved text and recordings in your browser; the paused screens do not submit those drafts. Clearing browser storage can remove unsaved drafts and access to an unsigned guest workspace. Work successfully saved to a signed-in account remains associated with that account.

Access and retention

Account-owned workspaces require sign-in. Video Feedback review links previously opened shared video and feedback for anyone with the link, and invited editors had to sign in to upload revisions. Those public screens now show a pause notice. Pausing the screens does not delete saved records or revoke existing sharing permissions. Authorised service operators and infrastructure providers may access information when needed to operate, support or secure the service.

Disqualifying a video annotation keeps it available for correction; it does not delete the record. In the annotation workspace, projects and videos moved to Trash can be restored for seven days, after which their files and annotations are permanently removed. Finalising a Video Feedback project could delete older video files after the owner's confirmation; the latest video, feedback and saved voice recordings were retained. Pausing Video Feedback does not itself delete any of that work. Expired temporary uploads are also cleaned up.

Retired tools and retained work

The drawing and image-annotation screens are retired. Existing image labels, drawings, object-related records and browser drafts are retained; retiring the screens does not delete them. The existing deletion controls described above still apply.

Used the old drawing tools? Email us and we'll export your work.

We will need to establish which work belongs to you before providing an export. Browser-only drafts remain on the original device and are not available from our database. Keep that browser's storage intact if you need help recovering them.

Your choices

You can edit annotations, export saved annotation work, use the available deletion controls and disconnect Google access through your Google account settings.

For your saved Video Feedback work, account deletion, other uploaded-data deletion requests or help recovering old work, email riya.shah@cheddarvox.com. We will need to establish which work belongs to you before providing it.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, email riya.shah@cheddarvox.com.

We will update this policy when our data practices change and show the updated date here.
