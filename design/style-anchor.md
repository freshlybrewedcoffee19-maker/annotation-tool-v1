Palette: warm cream background #fffdf8; deep navy ink and shirt #172033; pale blue office surfaces #dbeafe; cheddar yellow accents #ffcd38; warm orange accent #f59e0b. Avoid teal and dominant slate gray.

Mood: weary during repetitive clicking, expressive, relieved after voice takes over.
Never: 3D rendering, flat stock-vector people, stiff anatomy, plastic skin, sunny luxury office, gloomy gray wash, busy decoration, baked-in marketing copy, lens flare, neon.
